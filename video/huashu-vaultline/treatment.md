# Vaultline · 30-second launch film

## Product read

- Product: a Chinese-first secure AI banking workspace.
- Audience: banking decision-makers and hackathon judges evaluating safety, control and execution evidence.
- One thing to leave behind: **the model proposes; the bank server decides.**
- Personality: precise `+2`, premium `+2`, technical `+2`, institutional `+1`, energetic `+1`.
- Brief sentence: precise, premium, technical and institutionally controlled, with the restrained energy of an Apple keynote.

## Selected direction

**A · The Control Line** — interface-in-space + kinetic evidence.

The film follows one cobalt control line from natural-language intent into an action snapshot, an Action-bound MFA gate, a server receipt and the audit trail. The same line is the transition device and the product metaphor: nothing crosses it without server authorization.

Alternatives considered:

- **B · Receipt** — receipts and hashes become the typography; strong, but less legible for broad business coverage.
- **C · Everyday Intent** — cursor-led continuous UI demo; useful for a walkthrough, but too close to the previous product recording.

## Rhythm sheet

Tempo: `112 BPM`, 14 bars, 30 seconds.

| Stretch | Bars | Energy | Events / bar | Picture | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 1–2 | 7 | 3 | Real UI enters in space; “AI 能听懂，但不能替银行按键” | piano motif + sub pulse; first hit under 3 s |
| Intent | 3–4 | 7 | 3 | Prompt becomes an immutable action snapshot | hats enter; tick on each locked field |
| Authorization | 5–6 | 8 | 4 | MFA close-up; dots fill; “验证通过 ≠ 执行完成” | build; stop before the gate |
| Receipt | 7 | 6 | 2 | Teal server receipt lands | release; chime on receipt |
| Capabilities | 8–9 | 8 | 5 | Real screens for transactions, products, cards, subscriptions and liquidity | eighth-note montage, one hit per cut |
| Audit drop | 10–11 | 10 | 5 | Four server events lock onto one evidence rail | strongest drop; impact + subdrop |
| Security | 12 | 5 | 2 | Prompt injection is refused; no action created | one sharp cut + short silence |
| Brand | 13–14 | 3 | 1 | Mark, promise, hold | motif returns; final button |

## Continuity chain

`typed prompt → cobalt line → action snapshot edge → red MFA bracket → teal receipt line → amber/teal audit nodes → Vaultline mark`

Every transition carries the same line or a piece of the previous frame. No unrelated feature cards.

## Palette

- Reserve navy `#07111F` — ground, 52%.
- Platinum `#F5F7FB` — real UI and typography, 24%.
- Cobalt `#315BFF` — control line and primary action, 12%.
- Verified teal `#25B394` — receipts and evidence, 6%.
- Risk amber `#D7A04A` — confirmation, 4%.
- Verification red `#D15B52` — MFA and refusal, 2%.

All colours come from the current Vaultline product surface.

## Typography

- Chinese statements: Songti SC 600, deliberately editorial and institutional.
- UI and body: Noto Sans SC 500/700.
- English labels and IDs: Inter 600 and a mono fallback.

## Four hero stills

1. Frame 0: the real workspace already in motion, with “AI 能听懂。”
2. Action snapshot: `¥1,500.00` against a locked server-side snapshot.
3. Audit drop: four events locked to one evidence rail.
4. End mark: “模型提出计划。银行服务端决定结果。”

## Sound

Composer mode. Minimal-techno precision with piano and strings: a three-note motif, restrained sub pulse, one filtered build, one tape stop before authorization, and a resolved button on the mark. No narration; the film is muted-first and all essential meaning remains in the frames.
